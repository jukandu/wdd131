Product Review Site Plan

Joseph Silas Ukandu | WDD 131

Site Purpose & Target Audience

Purpose: The purpose of this website is to provide a user-friendly product review portal where customers can submit reviews and ratings for electronic and appliance products, helping future buyers make informed decisions.

Target Audience: Consumers looking for reliable product ratings, electronics enthusiasts, and everyday shoppers seeking detailed product feedback before purchasing.

Color Palette

The site uses a clean, professional color scheme suitable for an e-commerce review platform:

  • Primary Color: #008751
  • Secondary Color: #f4f4f9
  • Accent Color: #eab308
  • Text Color: #1f2937

Typography

Primary Font: System UI / Sans-serif for optimal readability and accessibility across devices.
